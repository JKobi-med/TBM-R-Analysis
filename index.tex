\section{Methods}\label{sec-data-methods}

\subsection{Study design}\label{study-design}

piano group and active control group

Blood samples were taken at three different time points. The first (T0)
one week before the weekly cycle of practical or theoretical music
lessons were started. The second (T1) after 6 months and the third (T2)
again after 6 months so one year after T0.

Because multiple tests were performed on each inidivuals time point (not
only blood samples but also fMRI as part of the original study) T0 fell
between March and June 2019, T1 between August 2019 and February 2020
and T2 between June 2020 and November 2020.

Auch in meiner Untersuchung? \emph{A small subgroup of participants were
recruited as early as in January 2019 (T0), and had their consecutive
examinations in August 2019 (T1) and in the end of February/beginning of
March 2020 (T2). In other words, their T2 was directly before the
lockdown started. Therefore, this small subgroup was excluded from the
main analyses}

\subsection{Study participants}\label{study-participants}

The participants of the study cohort were recruted at the Geneva Musical
Minds Lab, Geneva School of Health Sciences, University of Applied
Sciences and Arts Western Switzerland (HES-SO), Geneva, Switzerland and
the Hannover Medical School, Dept. for Clinical Psychiatry, Social
Psychiatry and Psychotherapy, Division of Clinical Psychology and Sexual
Medicine in corporation with the Institut für Musikphysiologie und
Musiker-Medizin Hochschule für Musik, Theater und Medien affiliated to
the Hochschule für Musik, Theater und Medien Hannover. Possibility for
participation was made public through newspaper advertisement and
posters in public places. Table~\ref{tbl-demographics} summarizes
relevant demographic information about the study cohort. Some
requirements had been to be met for volunteers to participate in the
study. They had to be in overall good health, right-handed, retired and
not relying on hearing aids.

\subsection{Measurement of DNA
methylation}\label{measurement-of-dna-methylation}

\subsubsection{Target Genes}\label{target-genes}

We focused on the primer regions of three genes commonly
neuroplasticity-associated and hypthecised being potentially
epigenetically modifiable by playing the piano: BDNF4, PSD95, MAPT
(Tau).

\subsubsection{DNA isolation}\label{dna-isolation}

Genomic DNA (gDNA) was isolated at the Institute of Molecular and
Translational Therapeutic Strategies of Hannover Medical School from 50
µL blood using the ``NucleoMag Blood 200 µl'' kit (MACHEREY-NAGEL GmbH
\& Co.~KG, Düren, Deutschland) in concordance with the instructions of
the manufacturer. The isolated gDNA was stored at -20 °C. The blood
samples have been received anonymized and prior to gDNA isolation. Then
the samples were additionaly randomized \emph{(by doing what?)} to
minimize potential batch effects. DNA samples were diluted in 96-well
plates to a fixed concentration of 10 ng@tbl-demographics/µl.

\subsubsection{Bisulfite Conversion}\label{bisulfite-conversion}

The gDNA of all samples was then bisullfite converted via the ``EZ-96
DNA Methylation-Lightning™ MagPrep'' kit (Zymo Research Corporation,
Irvine, CA, USA) also following the manufacturers instructions.

\subsubsection{Primer design}\label{primer-design}

With Ensembl and Geneious Pro 5.6.7 the primer regions of our target
genes were located and the sequences of the primer regions analyzed.
Aided by Biocalculator (metabion), NetPrimer (Premier Biosoft), Methyl
Primer Express v1.0 (Applied Biosystems) and Bisulfite Primer Seeker
(Zymo) we designed primers for PCR with bisulfite-converted DNA (see
Table~\ref{tbl-primers-pcr}) and sequencing primers
(Table~\ref{tbl-primers-seq}) - later used to prepare Sanger Sequencing
- and orderd these primers from metabion GmbH (Martinsried,
Deutschland).

\subsubsection{Gene Amplification}\label{gene-amplification}

The primers were dissolved in nuclease-free water. The proportions have
been predetermined by the manufacturer. The stock solution 100 pmol. It
was then used to derive a working solution of 20 pmol (1:5). Both
solutions were prexserved atg -20 °C. Amplification was then performed
together with HotStarTaq® PCR Master Mix Kit (Qiagen, Hilden,
Deutschland). The samples were then puriefed with the PCR purification
kit ``CleanNGS'' (CleanNA, Waddinxveen, Niederlande).

\subsubsection{Sanger Sequencing}\label{sanger-sequencing}

The purfied PCR prodcus were sequenced with the BigDye Terminator v3.1
Cycle Sequencing Kit (Applied Biosystems, Foster City, CA, USA) and th e
sequencing primers mentioned above (Table~\ref{tbl-primers-seq}). The
sequencing product were purifed with the ``CleanDTR'' kit (CleanNA,
Waddinxveen, Niederlande).

Then using the Applied Biosystems' 3500 Genetic Analyzer (ABI 3500xL)
Capillary electrophoresis was performed.

\subsubsection{Data Processing via ESME}\label{data-processing-via-esme}

The data derived from the ABI 3500xL was then processed with Epigenetic
Sequencing Methylation (ESME) analysis software.

\subsection{Data Structuring and Statistical
Analysis}\label{data-structuring-and-statistical-analysis}

For data structuring and transformation Excel, SPSS and R (4.5.2),
\emph{using the following packages: tidyverse, dplyr, readxl \ldots{}} .
After structuring the data, R (4.5.2) was used to analyze the data.
Principal Component Analysis (PCA) was done with the prcomp package
(\ldots). Descriptive statistics we perforemd using the \ldots{} Plots
were designed using the ggplot2 and \ldots{} package.

We performed modelling with 3 different types of models. Linear models.
The difference of 2 time points was modelled (T2 - T0, T1 - T0 and T2 -
T1) and after that used as the depenedet variable.

Generalized Linear model (GLM) with the \ldots{} package

Lastly Zero-Inflated Beta Distribution Regression Models with random
effects (ZIBR) were performed using the glmmTMB package for modelling
and DHARMa for residual diagnostics.

  umgekehrt (\emph{Boxplots mit CpG-Methylierung der Gene!})
\item
  \textbf{Lagemaß u. Streuungsmaß?}

\subsubsection{Group differences}\label{group-differences}

Our main goal we aimed to determine whether the degree of methylation
differed between the intervention (piano lessons) and control groups.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Bei BDNF zeigt sich eine leichte Differenz zwischen der Kontrollgruppe
  (Median: 0.07, Mean: 0.07326) und der Pianogruppe (Median: 0.064,
  Mean: 0.06598), welche eine niedrigere Metyhlierung (Median: -0.006,
  Mean: -0.00728) aufwies. \textbf{Streuungsmaße?}
\item
  Weiterhin zeigen sich für die arithmetischen Mittel von BDNF und PSD95
  zwei Modi.
\item
  Tau war unimodal.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Time differences}\label{time-differences}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Bei BDNF zeigt sich, dass sich die Mittelwerte (Arithmetisch und
  Median) von T0 zu T1 leicht absenken und zu T2 wieder über das Niveau
  von T0 ansteigen.
\item
  Bei Tau steigt das arithmetische Mittel der Methylierung leicht von T0
  zu T1 und hält das Niveau bei T2.
\item
  Bei PSD95 verbleibt der Median der Methylierung gleichbleibend bei 0,
  während das arithmetische Mittel der Methylierung von T0 zu T1 steigt
  aber bei T2 wieder auf das Ausgangsniveau zurückfällt.
